Privacy Policy

Cinema Machine, by Infinite Studio AI · Effective 12 July 2026

This policy explains what information Cinema Machine ("we", "us") collects when you use the service at cinema.infinitestudioai.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. Cinema Machine is an AI film studio: you bring story ideas and the service helps you develop them into scripts, artwork, voices and video.

What we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and a password, used to create and sign in to your account. We do not collect your name unless you include it in your content.
  • Your creative content. The films you build — story ideas, loglines, scripts, characters, artwork, voices, storyboards and video clips — is stored so it syncs across your devices and sessions.
  • Billing information. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number; we store your subscription tier, credit balance and Stripe customer reference so your plan works.
  • Usage basics. Standard technical logs (such as request timestamps and error logs) needed to run and secure the service. We do not run third-party advertising or cross-site tracking.

How your content is processed

When you use generation features (writing, images, voices, video), the relevant parts of your content — for example a scene description or a character reference image — are sent server-side to the AI model providers that power the studio (such as providers of text, image, voice and video models). These providers process the content to return the generated result. We send only what the feature needs, and your provider requests run through our servers — your account never exposes API keys in the browser.

Where your data lives

Account data and creative content are stored with our backend provider (Supabase), protected by row-level security so your projects are readable only by your account. Some working data (such as local drafts, preferences and render takes) is stored in your own browser and never leaves your device.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information or your creative work.
  • We don't use your creative content to train our own models.
  • We don't show ads or share your data with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Your account and content are kept while your account is active. If you delete a film, it is removed from your library; if you ask us to delete your account, we remove your account data and stored content within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons (such as invoices).

Your choices

  • You can delete individual films from your library at any time.
  • You can cancel your subscription at any time; your remaining credits stay usable.
  • You can request a copy or deletion of your data by emailing support@infinitestudioai.com.

Cookies and local storage

We use only what the service needs to work: a session token so you stay signed in, and browser local storage for preferences and drafts. No advertising or analytics cookies.

Children

Cinema Machine is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll note the new effective date here and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@infinitestudioai.com
